What winterization includes

How we winterize home Cape Cod-style — six steps, every visit.

One technician, two hours on average. Every step on the checklist, every visit, signed.

01

Shut the main & isolate

Main supply off at the curb stop or basement. Water heater isolated. Boiler shut down or set to vacation mode.

02

Drain every line

Open every faucet, drain the water heater and expansion tank, empty the supply lines from low points.

03

Compressed-air blow-out

Air-purge supply lines at low pressure to clear every drip — the part garden-hose drain methods miss.

04

Trap antifreeze pour

RV-grade non-toxic antifreeze in every p-trap, toilet bowl, and tank so nothing freezes and cracks the porcelain.

05

Outdoor & irrigation

Frost-free spigots drained, hoses pulled, irrigation backflow drained or coordinated with your irrigation contractor.

06

Written checklist signed

Every step initialed, photos attached, emailed to the homeowner and property manager before we leave.

What happens if you skip it

A burst pipe at a closed-up Cape Cod house is a $40,000 problem.

The Cape gets cold enough to freeze a half-inch supply line in an unheated wall. When that line splits, it doesn't drip — it sprays. And it sprays for however long it takes the neighbor, the alarm company, or the postal carrier to notice.

We've walked into seasonal homes in March where the entire first floor was destroyed because a $400 winterization wasn't done in November.

Why seasonal home plumbing Cape Cod is different

Cape Cod homes have quirks that catch off-Cape plumbers.

We've winterized hundreds of seasonal homes from Sandwich to Provincetown. Here's what makes them different from a typical year-round house.

~

Crawlspaces & outdoor showers

Most Cape homes have a low crawlspace or outdoor shower line that freezes first. We know where to look.

∥

Older galvanized & brass

Pre-1980 plumbing has fittings that crack under pressure if not fully drained. We pressure-down before air.

∇

Wells & well pumps

Off-town-water homes need the pressure tank drained and the well pump isolated — easy to miss.

▢

Rental turnover schedules

We coordinate around your last-rental-of-the-season date. No surprise visits while guests are still in the house.

⋯

Property manager handoffs

We share the checklist directly with your PM — and let them know when something looks like it needs attention before next season.

↻

Spring re-opens

We pre-book your spring opening at the same time. Pressurize clean, walk the house, hot water back on for the first guests.
