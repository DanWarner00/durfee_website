How they work

No ductwork. No fuel on-site. One refrigerant loop per zone.

A ductless mini split pairs a single outdoor compressor with one or more slim indoor heads. Instead of forcing heated or cooled air through ducts, refrigerant carries thermal energy directly to each room through small, insulated copper lines.

The result: room-by-room temperature control, near-silent operation, and meaningful efficiency gains over an old central system — without tearing into ceilings or losing closet depth to ducts.

  1. 01

    Single outdoor compressor

    One quiet unit on the side of the house drives 1–8 indoor zones.

  2. 02

    Refrigerant lines, not ducts

    3/8" insulated lines run through chases — no soffits, no demolition.

  3. 03

    Slim indoor heads per zone

    Each room sets its own temperature. Wall-mount, ceiling cassette, or floor units.

FAQ

Three questions Cape Cod homeowners ask before signing.

How long does a ductless mini split install take?+

Most Cape Cod retrofits land in 1–2 working days. A single-zone unit can be done in one day; a 4-zone whole-home system usually takes 2 days plus the commissioning walkthrough. We schedule the install only after the equipment is in our warehouse — no waiting on shipments mid-job.

Will a mini split actually keep up in January on Cape Cod?+

Cold-climate-rated ductless systems deliver full heating capacity well below 0°F — well past the coldest Cape Cod design temperature. A properly-sized system runs all winter without strip heat or a backup boiler.

How loud are the indoor heads?+

Quieter than a refrigerator. Modern wall-mount heads run 19–26 dB on low fan — most homeowners can't tell they're on. Bedrooms and second-home applications are a particularly good fit.
