FAQ

Two questions about full-project HVAC work.

Do you do full-system replacements as one project?+

Yes. We frequently scope projects where two or three pieces of equipment go in together — say, a heat pump conversion plus a HPWH plus a new thermostat. Doing them as one project means one plan, one rebate filing, and one install sequence.

Can I do it in phases?+

Yes. If the budget calls for phasing, we'll propose a sequence and quote each phase separately. The order usually matters — we'll walk you through what should happen first.
