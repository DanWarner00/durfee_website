FAQ

Three questions commercial accounts ask.

Do you handle annual RPZ backflow testing?+

Yes. We perform reduced-pressure-zone backflow testing and certification on an annual schedule for restaurants and small commercial accounts. We file the certification with the water authority and send you a copy.

Can you work on a maintenance schedule?+

Yes. We can set up a maintenance plan covering grease traps, RPZ testing, drain maintenance, and equipment tune-ups. Predictable scope, predictable cost.

What size commercial accounts do you take?+

We work best with restaurants, inns, retail, professional offices, and small commercial properties. For very large multi-tenant or industrial accounts, we'll be upfront if we're not the right fit.
