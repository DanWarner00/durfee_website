Cape Cod's Family-Owned Plumbing & Heating Team

Phil Durfee started this shop after a decade running service for a larger company and getting tired of watching homeowners get up-sold. Today, Durfee Plumbing & Heating is a small crew of licensed techs who actually answer the phone, and a plumber Cape Cod homeowners and property managers have trusted for more than 20 years.

Phil personally handles every service issue — if a job goes sideways, you talk to the owner, not a call center in another state. We're small on purpose. It's how we keep flat-rate pricing honest and the one-year warranty actually worth something.

Credentials & certifications

Licensed, certified, insured — provable on paper.

Ask any contractor for these documents before you let them touch your system. Ours are on file with the state and available on request.

MA

Master Plumber license

Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters. License held by Phil Durfee.

EPA

EPA Section 608 certified

Universal refrigerant handling certification for heat pump and A/C installation, recovery, and service.

Mass Save

Mass Save Heat Loan-approved

0% APR HEAT Loan financing and full rebate paperwork handled for qualifying heat-pump and water-heater work.

INS

Fully insured

General liability and workers' compensation on every job. Certificate of insurance available to property managers on request.
