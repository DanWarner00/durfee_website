Why book a duct cleaning

Three situations that genuinely warrant a clean.

❀

Allergies & asthma flare-ups

If indoor air quality is making symptoms worse, a duct clean removes built-up dust, pet dander, and debris from supply and return runs.

☁

Renovation or construction debris

Drywall dust, sanding particulate, and construction debris get kicked into the duct system. Cleaning before re-occupying keeps it out of the air.

↻

System hasn't been cleaned in years

If the system is more than a decade old and has never been professionally cleaned, a baseline service is worth the visit.

FAQ

Three honest questions about duct cleaning.

Does my home really need duct cleaning?+

Not always. If your system is well-maintained and no one in the home has air-quality issues, the EPA generally doesn't recommend routine cleaning. We'll tell you straight whether a clean is warranted after the inspection.

How often should ducts be cleaned?+

Most homes do well with cleanings every 5 to 10 years — sooner if there's been a renovation, water damage, or persistent allergy issues.

Will it really help my allergies?+

It can, especially if dust and debris have built up in the system. We can't promise specific health outcomes, but removing built-up debris from supply and return runs is a reasonable step toward better indoor air quality.
