What it is

A heat pump moves heat. It doesn't burn fuel to make it.

A modern cold-climate heat pump uses refrigerant and a compressor to move heat — pulling warmth out of outdoor air in winter and pushing heat out of your home in summer. One unit, two seasons, no fossil fuel on-site.

On Cape Cod, today's hyper-heat-rated systems work efficiently down to -13°F. They quietly replace an aging oil boiler or propane furnace — and cool the house the rest of the year.

  1. 01

    Outdoor unit collects heat

    Even at 5°F there's heat energy in outdoor air. The compressor concentrates it.

  2. 02

    Refrigerant carries it inside

    Insulated copper lines move the heat to indoor heads or an air handler.

  3. 03

    Indoor head delivers warm air

    Quiet, room-by-room temperature control. The same head cools in summer.

FAQ

What Cape Cod homeowners ask before signing.

Will a heat pump actually keep up on the coldest Cape Cod nights?+

Cold-climate-rated heat pumps deliver full heat capacity well below 0°F. Cape Cod winter design temperatures are warmer than that, so a properly-sized system will keep up without needing strip heaters or a backup boiler.

How much does heat pump installation Cape Cod–wide cost?+

Most whole-home retrofits land between $18,000 and $32,000 before rebates, depending on home size, zoning, and electrical work. The full $10,000 Mass Save rebate and 0% HEAT Loan financing can make the out-of-pocket far smaller. We quote flat-rate before any work begins.

Do I need to retire my oil or propane system to get the rebate?+

The maximum $10K Mass Save rebate is tied to whole-home installs that fully replace fossil-fuel heating. Partial installs that supplement existing heat receive smaller per-ton rebates. We'll tell you honestly which path makes sense for your home.

Can you install a ductless system in an older home without ducts?+

Yes — that's the most common Cape Cod scenario. We run insulated refrigerant lines through exterior chases and mount slim indoor heads on walls. No demolition, no soffits.

Who handles the Mass Save paperwork?+

We do — pre-qualification, post-install audit, and rebate submission. Sarah in our office files for every customer. You sign two forms; the check comes by mail.

What's the warranty?+

1-year parts and labor from Durfee on top of the manufacturer's 10–12 year parts warranty. If something fails, you call us — we deal with the manufacturer.
