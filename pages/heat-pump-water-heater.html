FAQ

Three questions Cape Cod homeowners ask before switching.

Does my basement need to be heated for a heat pump water heater to work?+

No — but the unit does need some air volume to draw heat from. We'll confirm during the in-home assessment. Most Cape Cod basements work fine; very tight utility closets may need a vented configuration.

Will I run out of hot water?+

Heat pump water heaters use the same tank-style storage you're used to. Recovery is a little slower than a fast-recovery gas tank but matches or beats most electric tanks. We size the unit to your household.

How does the Mass Save rebate work?+

If your install qualifies, we submit the pre-qualification and the post-install paperwork on your behalf. You sign two forms; the rebate check is mailed to you typically four to eight weeks after the audit clears. Final eligibility and rebate amount are subject to current Mass Save program rules.
