What we clear

Clog removal Cape Cod homes need — every line in the house.

Some clogs are a 15-minute snake job. Others want a camera and a jetter. We figure out which before we charge you.

⊙

Kitchen drains

Grease, coffee grounds, and dishwasher discharge build up at the elbow under the sink. Cleared with a sectional snake, p-trap rebuilt if needed.

○

Bathroom drains

Hair and soap. Showers, tubs, and bathroom sinks cleared from the access point — usually no need to pull the trap.

▢

Floor & basement drains

Floor drains back up when the main line is partially blocked. We camera-scope to find the real cause before snaking blind.

═

Sewer cleaning Cape Cod main lines

Main-line backups: snake to the obstruction, camera-confirm, then hydro-jet if it's grease or root intrusion. We won't sell a sewer dig you don't need.

Warning signs you need service

Six signs a small problem is about to become a big one.

↓

Multiple slow drains at once

One slow sink is a local clog. Two or three slow drains usually means the main line is partially blocked.

~

Gurgling toilets or sinks

Air escaping past a partial blockage. Common warning before a full backup.

!

Sewage smell indoors

Trap dried out, vent stack blocked, or sewer gas leaking past a damaged line. Get it diagnosed before it spreads.

↑

Water backing up where it shouldn't

Toilet flushes and the shower fills? That's main-line. Stop running water and call.

●

Recurring clogs in the same spot

Not bad luck — usually root intrusion or a belly in the line. A camera scope finds it in 10 minutes.

You've been pouring store-bought down for weeks

Chemical drain cleaners damage older pipe and the sealants in joints. Stop and call.

Store-bought drain cleaner

Caustic, blind, temporary.

  • × Lye-based chemistry burns through hair clogs by burning hair — and pipe joints.
  • × Sits on top of grease & sediment without dissolving the real cause.
  • × Sends caustic sludge into your septic or town main.
  • × Clog returns in days, often worse.
Durfee professional clear

Mechanical, monitored, lasting.

  • ✓ Sectional snake or hydro-jetter sized for the line, not for the shelf.
  • ✓ Camera scope confirms the real cause & verifies the clear.
  • ✓ Pipe and joint sealants stay intact.
  • ✓ Flat-rate, quoted before we start. No surprise bills.
