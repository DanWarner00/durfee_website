What Mass Save is — in plain English

A statewide program that pays you to upgrade.

Mass Save is the Massachusetts energy-efficiency program funded by every electric and gas utility customer in the state. You're already paying for it on your bill. The program returns that money to homeowners — as rebates and 0% APR loans — when they upgrade to more efficient heating, cooling, and water-heating equipment.

It is not a tax credit, not a guess, not a future promise. The check comes in the mail four-to-eight weeks after the post-install audit.

In one paragraph

You install a qualifying heat pump, mini split, or heat pump water heater with a participating contractor like Durfee. We pre-qualify the install with Mass Save before we touch a tool. We do the work. A Mass Save auditor inspects. We submit the rebate. You get a check.

FAQ

Three questions homeowners always ask.

Do I qualify for Mass Save rebates?+

If you pay an electric or gas bill in Massachusetts that has a Mass Save surcharge on it — almost everyone does — you're eligible to participate. Specific rebate amounts depend on the equipment you install, whether the install fully retires fossil-fuel heating, and current 2026 program rules. We'll tell you the exact rebate path on the consult call.

How long does the rebate process take?+

From first call to check in your mailbox is typically 8–12 weeks. The install itself runs 1–3 days. Pre-qualification can take 1–2 weeks. The post-install audit happens within 30 days of completion. The rebate check usually arrives 4–8 weeks after the audit clears.

What equipment brands qualify?+

For heat pumps and mini splits, the equipment must be on the AHRI cold-climate qualified list. We install AHRI-listed cold-climate equipment from the manufacturers that consistently perform on Cape Cod winters — we won't recommend equipment that voids your rebate. Ask us which system fits your home; we'll walk you through the qualifying options before you commit.
