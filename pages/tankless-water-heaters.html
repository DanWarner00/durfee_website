Why pick tankless

Three reasons busy Cape Cod households go tankless.

∞

Endless hot water

On-demand heating means consecutive showers, laundry, and dishwashing without running out. Good fit for 3+ bath homes and busy summer rental properties.

▢

Wall-mounted footprint

A tankless unit takes the floor space of a small shoebox on the wall, freeing up basement or utility-room storage where a 50-gallon tank used to sit.

↺

Lasts roughly twice as long

Tankless units typically last 20+ years versus 10-12 for a traditional tank — and there's no tank to spring a leak.

FAQ

Three questions about tankless on Cape Cod.

Will a tankless really keep up with multiple showers?+

Yes — that's the point. A correctly-sized unit handles two or three simultaneous draws (showers + dishwasher) without temperature drop. We size based on your household's peak demand, not a rule of thumb.

Does my existing gas line need to be upgraded?+

Sometimes. Tankless units pull more BTU instantaneously than a tank. We check the gas-line size during the assessment and quote any upgrade as part of the flat-rate. No surprise charges mid-job.

How much maintenance do they need?+

An annual descale (especially on Cape Cod where well water can run hard) keeps the heat exchanger running clean. We offer this as a one-off visit or as part of a maintenance plan.
