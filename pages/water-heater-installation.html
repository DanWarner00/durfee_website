Signs you're due for a replacement

Six warning signs we hear about every week.

10+

Tank is over 10 years old

Most tanks fail between year 10 and year 14. Replacing on your schedule is far cheaper than after a flood.

●

Rusty or discolored water

Brown, orange, or cloudy hot water usually means the anode rod is gone and the tank is corroding from the inside.

⌁

Popping or rumbling sounds

Sediment buildup hardens at the bottom of the tank. The popping is steam pockets forming under it.

~

Hot water runs out fast

If you can't get through one shower, the dip tube or heating element is going. We can confirm in 10 minutes on-site.

!

Visible leak or rust on the tank

Once the tank itself is leaking, it's done. Call us before it gives up entirely.

$

Electric bill keeps creeping up

Aging tanks lose efficiency. A hybrid heat pump unit can cut water-heating costs by ~70%.
